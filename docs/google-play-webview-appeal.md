# Google Play appeal: Webviews and Affiliate Spam

Use this as a working note if Google Play flags RotorReady for the Webviews and Affiliate Spam policy.

## What Google flagged

Google reported that the Android app appears to provide a WebView of `https://rotor-ready.com` and requested proof that the app publisher is allowed to use that website/content.

## Recommended evidence

Prepare a single PDF with formal proof. Screenshots, emails, and informal messages may not be accepted.

- A signed declaration on Mayday AS letterhead stating that Mayday AS owns/administers `rotor-ready.com` and authorizes the RotorReady Android app (`com.mayday.rotorready`) to load and display RotorReady content.
- Domain ownership evidence, such as registrar/DNS admin export, Google Search Console verification, or DNS TXT proof.
- Vercel/project ownership evidence showing `rotor-ready.com` is managed by Mayday AS.
- A short statement that RotorReady is not an affiliate/referral app, does not drive affiliate traffic, and does not earn referral commissions.

## Appeal text draft

Hello Google Play Policy Team,

RotorReady (`com.mayday.rotorready`) is the official Android app for RotorReady, published by Mayday AS. Mayday AS owns and administers the RotorReady web domain `https://rotor-ready.com` and the RotorReady training content served from that domain.

The app is not an affiliate, referral, or traffic-arbitrage app. It does not send users to third-party shopping, advertising, or affiliate destinations, and it does not earn referral commissions. It is a first-party helicopter-pilot training and repetition app containing RotorReady quiz, procedures, limitations, systems, and offline training content.

We have attached formal documentation proving that Mayday AS owns/administers `rotor-ready.com` and authorizes the RotorReady Android app (`com.mayday.rotorready`) to display and use that content in the app.

We have also updated the Android app to show a RotorReady-branded local error screen if the app cannot connect, instead of a generic WebView network error page.

Please reinstate the app and let us know if any additional documentation is required.

Regards,
Mayday AS / RotorReady
