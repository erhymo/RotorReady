DAILY OR PREFLIGHT CHECKS

Normal procedure

Remove ground handling wheels, covers and tiedowns; clear frost, ice or snow, especially from the rotor blades. Check general condition, fretting at rivets/seams, Telatemp indicators and torque stripes before working through the 14 station-by-station checks below.

CAUTION

Do not pull down on blades to teeter the rotor — to lower a blade, push up on the opposite blade.

CAUTION

Verify erosion on the lower surface of the main rotor blades has not exposed the skin-to-spar bond line.

CAUTION

Fill baggage compartments under unoccupied seats to capacity before using compartments under occupied seats; avoid objects that could injure an occupant in a hard landing.

CAUTION

Ensure all doors are unlocked before flight to allow rescue or exit in an emergency.

CAUTION

Remove left seat controls if the person in that seat is not a rated helicopter pilot.

1 — Upper forward cowl doors, right side
•
Battery switch ON; oil pressure, alt and aux fuel pump lights ON; push warning light test switches to test; check fuel gages; battery switch OFF.
•
Aux fuel tank quantity checked, filler cap tight, no leaks; fuel lines no leaks; fuel tank sump and gascolator drains sampled.
•
Gearbox oil full, no leaks; hydraulic system fluid full, no leaks; rotor brake actuation normal.
•
Flex coupling — no cracks, nuts secure; yoke flanges no cracks; gearbox/hydraulic pump Telatemps normal.
•
Control rod ends free without looseness; steel tube frame no cracks; all fasteners secure; tail rotor control no interference.
2 — Main rotor
•
Blades clean, no damage or cracks; pitch change boots no leaks; main hinge bolts have cotter pins installed.
•
All rod ends free without looseness; all fasteners secure; swashplate scissors no excessive looseness; upper forward cowl doors latched.
3 — Lower cowl door, right side
•
Air box and duct secure; engine sheet metal no cracks; fuel lines no leaks; oil lines no leaks or chafing.
•
Exhaust system no cracks; throttle linkage operable; cowl door latched.
4 — Aft cowl door, right side
•
Oil cooler door and V-belt condition checked; V-belt slack 1.5 in (4 cm) maximum; sprag clutch and upper bearing no leaks; Telatemp normal.
•
Sheave condition checked; flex coupling no cracks, nuts secure; yoke flanges no cracks; steel tube frame no cracks.
•
Tail rotor control no interference; tailcone attachment bolts checked; cowl door latched.
5 — Engine rear
•
Cooling fan nut pin in line with marks; cooling fan and fan scroll no cracks; tailpipe hanger no cracks.
6 — Empennage
•
Tail surfaces no cracks; fasteners secure; position light checked; tail rotor guard no cracks.
7 — Tail rotor
•
Gearbox Telatemp normal, oil visible, no leaks; blades clean, no damage or cracks; pitch links no looseness.
•
Teeter bearings checked, teeter bearing bolt does not rotate; control bellcrank free without looseness.
8 — Tailcone
•
Skins no cracks or dents; strobe light condition checked; antenna checked.
9 — Cowl door, left side
•
Engine oil 7–9 qts; oil filter secure, no leaks; battery and relay secure (if located here).
•
Steel tube frame no cracks; engine sheet metal no cracks; exhaust system no cracks; cowl door latched.
10 — Main fuel tank
•
Quantity checked; filler cap tight; no leakage.
11 — Fuselage, left side
•
Baggage compartments checked; removable controls secure if installed; collective controls clear.
•
Seat belts checked and fastened; doors unlocked and latched, hinge safety pins installed.
•
Landing gear checked; position light checked; static port clear.
12 — Nose section
•
Pitot tube clear; windshield condition and cleanliness checked; landing lights checked; yaw string checked.
13 — Fuselage, right side
•
Baggage compartments checked; seat belts checked and fastened; aft door unlocked and latched, hinge safety pins installed.
•
Landing gear checked; position light checked; static port clear.
14 — Cabin interior
•
Loose articles removed or stowed; instruments, switches and controls condition checked; clock functioning; adjustable pedal pins secure.

Source: POH Sect. 4 — Daily or Preflight Checks
