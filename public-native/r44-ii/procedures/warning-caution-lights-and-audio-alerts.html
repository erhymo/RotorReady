WARNING/CAUTION LIGHTS & AUDIO ALERTS

CWP light-by-light reference
Engine & powerplant
OIL
Loss of engine power or oil pressure. Check the engine tach for power loss; check the oil pressure gage and, if pressure loss is confirmed, land immediately — continued operation without oil pressure causes serious engine damage.
ENG FIRE
Possible fire in the engine compartment — see Engine Fire During Start / In Flight.
LOW FUEL
Approximately three gallons of usable fuel remaining; the engine will run out of fuel after ten minutes at cruise power. Do not use this as a working fuel-quantity indication.
AUX FUEL PUMP
Low auxiliary fuel pump pressure. Land as soon as practical; land immediately if accompanied by erratic engine operation.
FUEL FILTER
Fuel strainer contamination. Land as soon as practical; land immediately if accompanied by the aux fuel pump light or erratic engine operation.
Drive system
MR TEMP
Excessive main rotor gearbox temperature. Land immediately if accompanied by noise, vibration or temperature rise; otherwise land as soon as practical.
MR CHIP
Metallic particles in the main rotor gearbox. Same guidance as MR TEMP.
TR CHIP
Metallic particles in the tail rotor gearbox. Same guidance as MR TEMP; break-in fuzz can occasionally trigger this light.
CLUTCH
Clutch actuator engaging or disengaging — never take off before the light goes out. If it flickers or stays on in flight for more than 10 seconds, pull the CLUTCH breaker and land as soon as practical; reduce power and land immediately if other drive-system symptoms are present.
BRAKE
Rotor brake is engaged — release immediately in flight or before starting the engine.
Electrical & rotor speed
ALT
Low voltage / possible alternator failure. Turn off nonessential electrical equipment and cycle ALT off then on after one second to reset the control unit; land as soon as practical if the light stays on.
STARTER ON
Starter motor engaged. If the light doesn't go out when the starter button is released, immediately pull mixture off and turn the battery switch off; have the starter serviced.
LOW RPM (light + horn)
Rotor speed below 97%. Immediately lower collective, roll throttle on and, in forward flight, apply aft cyclic. Disabled when collective is full down.
HIGH RPM WARBLE
Rotor speed approaching the 108% limit — raise collective as required to control RPM.
GOV OFF
Engine RPM governor is switched off.
Cabin & other
CARBON MONOXIDE
Elevated CO in the cabin — shut off the heater, open nose and door vents; if hovering, land or transition to forward flight; land immediately if CO-poisoning symptoms (headache, drowsiness, dizziness) accompany the light.
FULL THROTTLE (if installed)
Engine near full throttle — the governor cannot increase throttle further; lower collective as required to extinguish the light.
HYD (if installed)
Hydraulic system is switched off.
Note

If a light causes excessive glare at night, the bulb may be unscrewed or the circuit breaker pulled to eliminate glare during landing.

For chip lights: if no metal chips or slivers are found on the detector plug, clean and reinstall (refill the tail rotor gearbox with new oil) and hover for at least 30 minutes — replace the gearbox before further flight if the light returns.

Source: POH Sect. 3 — Warning/Caution Lights, Audio Alerts
