CLEAR AREA TAKE-OFF

Category A — Supplement 12, Part F

Running/rolling-style Category A take-off from a clear area. TDP is 30 ft AGL; from a 5 ft hover the aircraft accelerates on a nose-down attitude change to reach VTOSS (50 KIAS) by TDP.

CAUTION

If this procedure is modified, it may not be possible, if an engine fails in the take-off path, to carry out a safe OEI landing or achieve the scheduled OEI performance.

1
LDG LT & LDG LT2 switches — As required
2
Rotor speed — Set 102% NR
3
HEATER/ECS ENVIRO (if fitted) — Confirm SOV 1 & 2 switches selected to NORMAL, cycle HTR/ECS CONTROL switch to check ITT rise and HEATER ON advisory, then select back OFF
4
Power checks — Carry out as required per the Engine Power Checks procedure, Section 4K Performance Data
5
Pilot Altimeter — Set 0 ft, or nearest 1000 ft setting to T-O altitude, with collective at MPOG
6
PARK BRAKE — Confirm released
7
Hover — Establish a 5 ft (1.5 m) AGL hover
8
Flight controls — Check correct functioning
9
MFD PWR PLANT page — Check all parameters within normal operating limits
10
Warnings and Cautions — Confirm none displayed
11
PI — Check PI’s matched, NR 102%. Note PI value
12
Collective/Cyclic Control — Apply cyclic to commence a nose-down attitude change to -5° and maintain; at approximately half way through the rotation apply collective to increase PI by 18% above hover value (or to 110% if 18% is not available)
13
Take-Off Decision Point (TDP) — At 30 ft AGL (TDP), continue acceleration; verify VTOSS (50 KIAS) already achieved
14
Acceleration/Climb — Adjust pitch attitude to approximately 5° nose up and continue climb up to 200 ft AGL
15
Landing gear — UP (when reaching VY but not before 200 ft AGL)
16
NR/NF — Select 100% at VY
17
Power — Adjust collective to continue climb at VY, utilizing up to Take-Off power as required, to 1000 ft
18
After Take-Off checks — Complete
19
At 1000 ft AGL — Adjust collective and cyclic to continue climb at VY or cruise speed as required
20
HEATER/ECS ENVIRO (if fitted) — As required
21
LDG LT & LDG LT2 switches — OFF, if used
22
Pilot Altimeter — Set as required
Note

OAT conditions close to 50°C may produce ITT-limited PI, in HIGE, above 92% PI. Under these conditions using 110% PI (even though the change in PI is less than 18%) produces the power required to guarantee CAT A performance.

Source: RFM Supplement 12, Part F — Section 2F Normal Procedures
