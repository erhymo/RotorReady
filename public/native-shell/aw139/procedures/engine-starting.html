ENGINE STARTING

Normal procedure
WARNING

Failure to follow the abort procedure may cause damage to the engine.

CAUTION

Ensure the second engine engages as NF reaches FLIGHT condition. A failed engagement shows as positive NF with near-zero torque — shut down the non-engaged engine first, then the other once stopped. A hard engagement means shut down both engines for maintenance.

Aborted engine start — recognize and abort
•
Light-up not within 10 seconds of ENG MODE to IDLE, abnormal noise, ITT beyond limits (HOT START caution), NG stagnation below 54%, main rotor not turning by NG 40%, or starter not disengaged by 49% NG — abort the start
1
ENG MODE switch — OFF (if engine does not shut down, ECL — OFF)
2
FUEL PUMP — OFF
3
ENG FUEL switch — OFF
Normal engine start — Engine 1
1
ENG 1 FUEL switch — ON, fuel valve indicator bar vertical
3
FUEL PUMP 1 switch — ON, 1 FUEL PUMP caution out, check pressure
4
ENG 1 MODE switch — IDLE
5–7
Gas producer (NG), ITT and engine oil pressure — Note increasing, rising as expected
8
Engine N°1 starter — Disengaged by 49% NG
9
Main hydraulic system — Confirm rise in pressure as the main rotor begins to rotate; cyclic centralized on PFD indicator
10
N1 engine power turbine speed (NF) and rotor speed (NR) — Confirm both stabilized at IDLE speed of 65%±1%
11
Engine and transmission oil — Check pressures and temperatures within limits
12
ENG 1 MODE switch — FLT; confirm GEN 1 ON (reset if required)
Engine 2 start — repeat the sequence for engine N°2
14–15
ENG 2 FUEL switch — ON; FUEL PUMP 2 switch — ON, check pressure
16
GEN 1 — Check loadmeter in green band (if external power not used)
17
Repeat the normal start sequence above for engine N°2
After both engines running
19–21
External power — OFF and disconnect if used; GEN 1 and GEN 2 — confirm ON; BUS TIE — confirm AUTO
22–24
RAD MSTR switch — ON; clock — set; rotor speed — confirm 100%
Note

Either engine may be started first; normal starts should be made in AUTO mode.

If engine N°2 is started first, set BUS TIE to ON and confirm MAIN BUS 2 voltage is not below 23V.

Source: RFM Sect. 2 — Engine Starting, Aborted Engine Start, Normal Engine Start
