COCKPIT/SAFETY CHECKS & ENGINE STARTING

Normal procedure
Cockpit/safety checks
1
Cockpit fire extinguisher — Secure
2
Pedals and seats — Adjust
3
Seat belts — Fasten and adjust
4
Circuit breakers — IN
5
Rotor brake — OFF, or BRAKE for windy conditions
6
Static source — NORMAL and guarded
7
ELT switch (if applicable) — Confirm ARM
8
EPGDS panel switches — OFF; ENG 1 & 2 MODE switches — OFF; RCP panel switches — NORM
9
APU PNL SEL MODE switch — OFF; ECS PNL HEATER — OFF; ICS panel mode switch — NORM
10
LDG GEAR lever — Confirm DOWN; PARK BRAKE lever — As required
Engine pre-start checks (APU)
1
BATT MASTER — ON
2
APU — Confirm green STATUS READY light, select SEL MODE ON, wait 2 seconds and confirm no FAIL message, then momentarily START and release to ON. Confirm green START then ON lights (APU GEN online in approx. 40–45 seconds)
3
MAIN BATT — ON; BATT AUX (if fitted) — ON
4
ECDU — Confirm PBIT IN PROGRESS, then LIGHTS page on completion; action any LOCKED CB LIST as required
5
ECDU LIGHTS page — POS LT and A/COLL ON; CAB SIGN ON if required
6
ENG FIRE PANEL — Confirm guards closed, FIRE EXTING switch centred
7
RCP panel switches — Confirm NORM; AFCS AP1/AP2 — confirm not engaged
8
MISC PNL — 1 & 2 ENG A/ICE PROTECTION OFF; EMERG LTS ON then ARM
9
Cyclic — centred (green PFD indicator); collective — full down; LDG GEAR panel — 3 greens, EMER DOWN switch secure and guarded
10
PARK BRAKE — Pull and turn, confirm PARK BRAKE ON advisory
11
ECDU FIRE test (6R) — Confirm ENG 1, ENG 2, APU and BAG FIRE audio/voice warnings and CAS/MWL indications in sequence
12
LAMP TEST — Confirm FIRE/ARM, LDG GEAR, AFCS, ECDU and APU panel lamps illuminate
13
ENG INTK TEST (if OAT below 5°C) — Select 1 & 2 ENG A/ICE PROTECTION ON, run ENG INTK TEST and confirm INTAKE FAIL cautions during test then clearing after
14
Aural Warning short and long test — Confirm MWL/MCL illuminate and the full aural warning sequence is heard
15
TRANSMISSION OIL TEST — Confirm MGB/IGB/TGB OIL LOW cautions and MCL
16
ECDU HYD (6R) — ELEC PUMP ON, carry out full-and-free cyclic/collective/pedal check one control at a time, then ELEC PUMP OFF, confirm HYDRAULIC SOV NORM
17
Rotor Brake — OFF, confirm no ROTOR BRAKE ON advisory
Engine starting
1
MFD — Confirm PWR PLANT page; FUEL PUMP 1 & 2 — confirm ON; FUEL ENG 1 & 2 SOV — OPEN; FUEL XFEED — AUTO
2
Engine temperature (ITT) — Confirm less than 150°C
3
ENG 1 MODE switch — IDLE (when ITT below 150°C and NG is 0%)
4
NG — Note increasing and START legend displayed; ITT — note increasing and IGN legend displayed; oil pressure — confirm rising
5
Engine 1 starter — Disengaged by 52% ±2% NG; confirm hydraulic pressure rise and cyclic centralized as rotor begins to turn
6
NF/NR — Confirm stabilized at IDLE speed 55% ±1%
7
Repeat items 2–6 for ENG 2 MODE switch, confirming NF/NR stabilizes at 73% ±1% with both engines at idle
8
AFCS panel — Press TEST and follow MFD AFCS synoptic instructions; confirm successful completion
9
APU — SEL MODE OFF once main engines established (STATUS CLDWN caution during ~70 second shutdown)
After engine start checks
1
If OAT below 5°C — carry out the Engine Anti Ice Bleed Valve check on each engine (stabilize NG 90–95%, confirm ITT rise of at least 30°C with A/ICE PROTECTION ON, confirm reduction OFF)
2
ENG 1 & 2 MODE switches — FLT, confirm NR/NF stabilized at 102%
3
MISC PNL — ENG and INTAKE ANTI ICE as required
4
Fuel cross-feed test — Cycle FUEL PUMP 1 OFF/ON and FUEL PUMP 2 OFF/ON with XFEED CLSD/OPEN/AUTO, confirming pressure and CAS indications at each step
5
MFD ELECTRIC synoptic — Confirm MAIN and AUX batteries not discharging; ECDU ELEC — confirm GEN 1 & 2 ON, TRU 1 & 2 ON, NON ESS 1 & 2 AUTO, BTC 1 & 2 CLSD
6
MFD HYDRAULIC synoptic — Confirm pressure and temperature within limits with small control inputs and no pressure drop
7
MFD PWR PLANT page — Check all engine parameters within limits, NR/NF 102%
8
Altimeters (Pilot, Standby, Copilot) — Set and cross-check; RAD ALT — confirm zero altitude (±5 ft), test button confirms RA1/RA2 50 ft
9
DH selector, SVS/FD SEL/EVS, MCDU COMM/NAV, ICS panels — Set as required
10
ECDU PITOT — Confirm AUTO; APU — confirm green STATUS READY, no APU ON advisory

Source: RFM Sect. 2A — Cockpit/Safety Checks, Engine Pre-Start Checks (APU), Engine Starting
