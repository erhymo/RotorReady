GROUND / ELEVATED HELIPORT VERTICAL TAKE-OFF

Category A — Supplement 4, Part A

Vertical Category A take-off from a ground level or elevated heliport/helideck. TDP is 110 ft ATS, reached from a 7 ft ATS hover before rotating to accelerate through VTOSS (50 KIAS).

CAUTION

If this procedure is modified, it may not be possible, if an engine fails in the take-off path, to carry out a safe OEI landing or achieve the scheduled OEI performance.

1
Climb Out Safety Speed — Select VCOSS based on reported headwind component
2
PARK BRAKE — Apply; confirm pressure on brake pedals and PARK BRAKE ON advisory illuminated
3
HEATER — As required
4
Pilot Altimeter — Set 0 ft or nearest 1000 ft setting to T-O altitude, collective at MPOG
5
Rad Alt — Check
6
Power checks — Confirm HEATER OFF, press PWR CHECK on MFD P-PLANT page, confirm positive power margin each engine
7
NOSEWHEEL lock — LOCK
8
Engine/Rotor — TQ/ITT matched as required, check NF/NR 102%
9
MFD PWR PLANT page — Check all parameters within normal limits, cross-check with PFD
10
Warnings and Cautions — None/as required
11
Flight controls — Check correct functioning
12
Hover — Establish a 7 ft ATS hover, no winds from rear sectors (090°–270°)
13
Collective/Cyclic Control — Increase PI to climb slowly to TDP (110 ft ATS), maintaining hover position
14
Take-Off Decision Point (TDP) — Maintain TDP (110 ft ATS) until ready to depart, noting pitch attitude
15
Hover departure — Rotate nose down slowly for a 5° attitude change, maintaining collective position; accelerate to VTOSS (50 KIAS), then continue climb and accelerate to VY
16
Climb — At VY adjust attitude to stabilize speed, continue climb
17
Landing gear — UP (when reaching VY but not below 200 ft ATS)
18
Power — Adjust collective to continue climb at VY (80 KIAS), using up to 5 min power as required, to 1000 ft ATS
19
At 1000 ft ATS — Adjust collective and cyclic to continue climb at VY or accelerate to cruise speed as required
20
PARK BRAKE — Release; confirm PARK BRAKE ON advisory not illuminated
21
After Take-Off checks — Complete

Source: RFM Supplement 4, Part A — Section 2A Normal Procedures
