Procedure

DOUBLE ENGINE FAILURE — AUTOROTATION

Both engines have failed. Warning Unit ROTOR RPM light and warning tone (NR low). Yawing motion nose left as NR and both N2 decrease; VMS shows power loss on both engines.

HIGE (hover in ground effect)
1
— Right pedal — Apply as necessary to stop yaw.
2
— Landing attitude — Establish.
3
— Collective lever — Raise to cushion landing.
In flight
— Autorotation — Perform.
Autorotation glide
1
— Collective lever — Reduce to maintain NR within limits (chevrons on FLI indicate direction of corrective movement).
2
— Airspeed — Vy.
3
— If situation permits: EMER SHD BUS sw — Lift guard, ON.
4
— Double engine emergency shutdown — Perform.
5
— Perform in-flight restart, if situation permits.
Flare and landing
~100 ft AGL
— Flare attitude — Establish (approx. 15° to 20°) to reduce forward speed and rate of descent; control NR.
~8-12 ft AGL
— Flare attitude — Reduce to approx. 10°.
Then
— Heading — Maintain; Collective lever — Raise to stop descent and cushion landing.
After landing
— BAT MSTR sw — OFF.
Note

Maximum range airspeed: 90 KIAS. Minimum rate-of-descent airspeed: 60 KIAS. Normal target: Vy.

Adjust flare height/attitude per actual gross mass, wind, and terrain.

Source: FLM BK117 D-2 Sect. 3.8.1 / 3.8.4.2

H145 D2 training reference. For training use only. Always cross-check with the latest approved Flight Manual.
