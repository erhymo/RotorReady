Procedure

BEFORE STARTING ENGINES & ENGINE START

Normal procedure
CAUTION

Avoid prolonged periods with Nr between 36–49% and 56–67%.

CAUTION

Restarting a hot engine that was not properly cooled for 2 minutes may cause a bowed compressor rotor and rubs of airfoils and seals — the damage is cumulative and may result in future hot starts.

Before starting engines
1.Seat & harness
— Seat and pedals — adjust; shoulder harness locks — check; parking brake — set.
2.Egress handles
— Pilot and copilot emergency egress handles — full forward and safetied.
3.Center console
— REVERSION CONTROL panel — NORM; OEI TRAIN — OFF; LDG GEAR handle — DN; EMER DN — UP; backup attitude indicator — caged.
4.Side & overhead console
— Compass — slaved; audio panels set; rotor brake — OFF; ECS CONT PANEL — OFF; FIRE DET/EXTG TEST — NORM; SERVO BST — ON, SERVO — BOTH; engine ignition — AUTO; fuel prime — AUTO.
5.Power sources
— APU CTRL/GEN — OFF; battery — OFF; AC/DC external power — OFF; DC CONV BACKUP — NORM; DC CONV NO. 1 and NO. 2 — ON; AC GEN NO. 1 and NO. 2 — OFF.
6.Circuit breakers
— IN.
Cockpit equipment checks
1.Battery & power
— BATT — ON; AC EXT PWR — as required.
2.Fire detection test
— Cycle INBD / NORM / OUTB / SMOKE, confirming FIRE/ARMED pushbutton illumination and aural alerts at each position, then return to NORM.
3.APU start
— AIR SRCE HEAT/START — ENG; wait at least 15 seconds for the APU GCU preflight test; APU CTRL — ON; APU GEN — ON.
4.Avionics
— CMFDs, RTUs and FMS — ON and set; compass — slaved and set; flotation system test once daily if over-water flight is planned.
5.Flight controls
— Hydraulic GND PUMP — ON; with SERVO on NO. 1 then NO. 2, move cyclic, collective and pedals through full range checking for freedom of movement and no binding; repeat with SERVO BST OFF to confirm full range with increased force; SERVO BST — ON, SERVO — BOTH.
6.AFCS check
— Center all flight controls; SAS 1 & 2 and AP 1 & 2 — ON; rotor brake — ON; disengage then re-engage YAW/CYCLIC/COLL TRIM; run the Preflight Built-In Test via AFCS MODE SEL — TEST; AP PWR RESET — press AP 1 and AP 2 buttons.
7.Hydraulic leak test
— Rotor brake — ON; select LDI on the HEALTH & STATUS page; confirm LDI PASS.
8.Trim & MGB pressure
— GND PUMP — OFF; YAW/CYCLIC/COLL TRIM — ON; SAS 1 & 2 — ON/PRI; AP 1 & 2 — ON/ATT; backup attitude indicator — TEST then ARM; on EICAS SHOW OBE, confirm a functioning MGB oil pressure gauge (no red dashes) and that the MGB OIL PRES warning illuminates, then HIDE OBE.
Starting engines
1.Setup
— NO. 1 and NO. 2 FUEL — as required; ENG IGN NO. 1 and NO. 2 — AUTO; rotor brake — as required; fire guard posted if available; rotor blades — clear.
2.No. 1 engine
— Throttle — STOP; NO. 1 ENG START button — press; throttle — IDLE when TGT is below 150°C.
3.No. 2 engine
— Throttle — STOP; NO. 2 ENG START button — press; throttle — IDLE when TGT is below 150°C.
Note

Both engines may be started simultaneously when OAT is below 25°C at sea level, decreasing the allowable OAT by 2°C per 1000 ft of altitude.

Do not take off if red dashes are shown in place of an MGB pressure indication, or if the MGB OIL PRES warning does not illuminate during the static check.

Source: S-92 RFM Part 1, Section II — Before Starting Engines, Cockpit Equipment Checks, Starting Engines
